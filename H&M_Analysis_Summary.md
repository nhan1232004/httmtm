# H&M Group Analysis Summary

## Overview
**Title:** Phân tích về tập đoàn H&M (Analysis of H&M Corporation)

**Full Name:** H & M Hennes & Mauritz AB

This analysis examines H&M's corporate structure, business model, organizational management, product categories, supply chain, and strategic approach, with examples applicable to Vietnam market context.

---

## 1. Corporate Governance Structure

### Board of Directors
- **Board Composition:** 3-12 members elected at Annual General Meeting (AGM), including employee representatives
- **Board Chair (2024):** Karl-Johan Persson
- **Function:** Ultimate responsibility for corporate organization and group management
- **Objective:** Bridge family business heritage with modern management practices

### Oversight Committees

**Audit Committee:**
- Monitors financial report integrity
- Ensures effectiveness of internal control systems
- Manages risk oversight
- Primary communication channel between Board and independent auditors

**Nomination Committee:**
- Proposes board member candidates
- Determines appropriate compensation levels
- Ensures diversity of skills and independence among members

---

## 2. Executive Management Structure

### EMT (Executive Management Team)
- Responsible for strategic direction and major corporate decisions
- Members work to drive business results and track progress based on long-term objectives
- Key focus: Performance management and target achievement

### Diversity Commitment
- Executive leadership composition: 5 women and 7 men (2026)
- **65% of leadership positions** across the group are held by women
- Reflects company commitment to gender equality

---

## 3. Organizational Model: Multi-brand Matrix Organization

H&M uses a dual-axis matrix structure combining central functions with individual brand flexibility.

### Brand Axis (Brand-Based Organization)
Each brand operates as an independent business unit with distinct positioning:

#### Primary H&M Brands:
- **H&M** - Main revenue driver, fashion-focused
- **H&M HOME** - Home furnishings line
- **H&M Beauty** - Beauty and personal care
- **H&M Move** - Active/sports apparel

#### Portfolio Brands (Specialist Segments):
- **COS** - Contemporary, minimalist fashion
- **Weekday** - Casual, trend-focused (integrating Monki and Cheap Monday)
- **& Other Stories** - Individual, distinctive style
- **ARKET** - Premium casual wear
- **Singular Society** - Niche/specialty collection

**Structure:** Each brand has its own Managing Director responsible for P&L and brand positioning

### Functional Axis (Centralized Functions)
Group Functions support all brands through:

**IT & Data:**
- Shared e-commerce platform infrastructure
- Supply chain management systems

**Logistics:**
- Warehouse and transportation management across multi-brand network
- Distribution centers automated for 30% faster order processing
- Omnichannel fulfillment (physical stores + online)

**People Organization:**
- Core values development
- Unified cultural standards across group

**Sustainability Development:**
- Environmental standards for brands
- Supplier compliance requirements

## 4. Key Business Functions

### Product Development & Design

**Designers:**
- Trend forecasting
- Color palette selection
- Collection creation for different age groups and styles

**Production Liaison:**
- Bridge between design and manufacturing
- Ensures optimal quality, competitive cost, and safety compliance

**Quality & Pattern Specialists:**
- Technical specification accuracy verification
- Product durability testing before mass production

### Purchasing & Sourcing (Strategic Advantage Area)

**Key Characteristics:**
- **Global footprint:** 20+ production offices worldwide
- **Asset-light model:** No factory ownership
- **Partner model:** Strategic relationships with independent manufacturers

**Supplier Management:**
- Supplier Life Cycle Management (evaluation and selection based on production capability, transparency, sustainability commitment)
- On-site sustainability specialists at manufacturing hubs
- Carbon reduction and worker rights improvement initiatives
- Shift toward "demand-driven" supply chain model with circular economy principles

**Distribution Centers (DC):**
- Highly automated for omnichannel fulfillment
- New DCs have improved order processing by up to 30%
- Support both physical retail and online orders

**Last-Mile Logistics:**
- Route optimization for transportation efficiency

## 9. Strategic Recommendations

Based on H&M's model, key strategic insights include:

### For Retail Operations:
1. **Multi-brand portfolio strategy** - Different brands for different customer segments maximizes market penetration
2. **Asset-light manufacturing** - Partner model reduces capital requirements and increases flexibility
3. **Centralized functions, decentralized brands** - Balances scale economies with brand autonomy

### For Supply Chain:
4. **Global sourcing network** - 20+ production offices enable cost optimization and supply chain resilience
5. **Demand-driven approach** - AI-powered forecasting reduces excess inventory
6. **Sustainability integration** - Environmental and social compliance as competitive advantage

### For Digital Transformation:
7. **Omnichannel integration** - Seamless online-offline experience critical for modern retail
8. **Advanced analytics** - Data-driven decision making across operations
9. **Technology as enabler** - Not a side function but central to business strategy

### For Organizational Culture:
10. **Values-driven culture** - Clear, simple principles guide decision-making across global organization
11. **Diversity and inclusion** - 65% of leadership by women sets benchmark for industry
12. **Continuous learning** - Career development across functions and geographies

## 10. Vietnam Market Implications

H&M's multi-brand matrix approach offers lessons for ShopVN:

- **Segment expansion:** Create specialized brands/categories for different Vietnamese consumer segments
- **Supply chain localization:** Develop Vietnam-based supplier relationships
- **Technology investment:** Build advanced demand forecasting and inventory management
- **Cultural adaptation:** Maintain core values while adapting to local market preferences
- **Omnichannel excellence:** Integrate online and physical retail experiences
- **Sustainability focus:** Align with growing Vietnamese consumer environmental consciousness

---

## Summary

H&M demonstrates how a global retail corporation:
- Maintains brand consistency through centralized functions
- Enables brand differentiation through decentralized management
- Reduces capital investment through strategic supplier partnerships
- Leverages technology for competitive advantage
- Creates cohesive organizational culture across diverse markets and brands

The matrix organization model balances scale economics with market responsiveness—a valuable blueprint for retail companies operating across multiple market segments and geographies.
